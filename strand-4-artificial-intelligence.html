Strand 4 - Chapter 5

Artificial Intelligence

AI is the part of computing that tries to make machines perform tasks that normally need human intelligence. This chapter explains the main AI ideas that appear in the syllabus and the mock papers.

What Artificial Intelligence Means

Artificial intelligence is the ability of a machine or computer system to perform tasks such as learning, reasoning, recognition and decision making.

  • AI does not mean the computer thinks exactly like a human.
  • It means the computer can imitate some intelligent behaviour.

Main AI Areas in the Syllabus

  • Machine learning: systems learn patterns from data.
  • Artificial neural networks: models inspired by how brains are linked.
  • Expert systems: rule-based systems that use stored knowledge.
  • Virtual reality, augmented reality and mixed reality: technologies that blend or create digital environments.

Machine Learning and Neural Networks

  • Machine learning allows computers to improve performance after learning from examples or data.
  • Artificial neural networks are made of connected units that process patterns.
  • These ideas help with speech recognition, image recognition and prediction.

Expert Systems

  • An expert system stores knowledge and uses rules to reach decisions.
  • It often uses IF-THEN rules.
  • Example: a medical support system that asks questions and suggests likely conditions.

Simple Rule

IF a patient has fever and cough, THEN suggest checking for a respiratory infection.

Weak AI and Strong AI

  • Weak AI handles a limited task, like voice assistants or spam filters.
  • Strong AI is the idea of a machine with broad human-like intelligence across many tasks.

Human, Animal and Machine Intelligence

  • Humans reason widely and adapt flexibly.
  • Animals are intelligent in survival and natural behaviour.
  • Machines are fast, accurate and consistent but depend on data and design.

Ethics and Data Use

  • AI depends heavily on data, so privacy matters.
  • Poor data can lead to poor or unfair results.
  • People should think about safety, bias and correct use of AI tools.
  • AI should assist people, not be trusted blindly.

Exam-safe Summary

Artificial intelligence helps computers perform tasks that need learning or decision making. Machine learning helps them learn from data, neural networks help them recognise patterns, and expert systems use stored rules to make decisions.
