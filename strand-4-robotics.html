What a Robot Is

  • A robot is a programmable machine that can perform tasks automatically or semi-automatically.
  • Robots often use sensors to detect conditions and a controller to process instructions.
  • Robotics combines computing, electronics and mechanical movement.

Common Uses of Robots

  • Manufacturing and packaging
  • Surgery and healthcare support
  • Transport and delivery
  • Laboratory research
  • Photography and inspection
  • Working in dangerous places

Parts of a Robot

Part Function
Controller The brain of the robot; runs the program
Sensors Collect information such as light, motion or temperature
Actuators / Mechanical parts Carry out movement, lifting, turning or gripping
Easy memory hook Sensor = notices. Controller = decides. Actuator = acts.

Advantages and Disadvantages

Advantages Disadvantages
Fast and accurate Expensive to buy and maintain
Can work repeatedly without getting tired May replace some jobs
Useful in dangerous environments Can fail, malfunction or be hacked

Asimov's Three Laws of Robotics

  1. A robot must not harm a human being, or allow a human being to come to harm.
  2. A robot must obey human orders unless those orders conflict with the first law.
  3. A robot must protect itself unless that conflicts with the first or second law.

One Good Essay Sentence

A robot uses sensors to collect information from its environment, a controller to process that information and actuators to perform actions.
