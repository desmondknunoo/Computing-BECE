Computational Thinking, broken into full chapters.

Strand 4 is the most detailed part of this revision site. Instead of squeezing everything into one page, it is divided into connected chapters so learners can understand the ideas step by step: programming, data types, algorithms, flowcharts, pseudocode, robotics and artificial intelligence.

Why Strand 4 Feels Hard

  1. It uses many new terms quickly.
  2. It mixes theory with step-by-step problem solving.
  3. Students often memorise names without practising the logic.
Best method: read one chapter, do one worked example, then try one practice question before moving to the next chapter.

Three Exam Mistakes to Avoid

  • Calling every text value a string, even when it is a truth statement.
  • Forgetting the difference between sequence, selection and iteration.
  • Writing algorithms as loose sentences instead of clear steps.

Best Study Order

  • Read Chapter 1 and practise data types.
  • Read Chapter 2 and draw symbols by hand.
  • Read Chapter 3 and write pseudocode for daily tasks.
  • Finish with Robotics and AI.
