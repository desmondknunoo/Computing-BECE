Policy Details

Privacy and Data Use

The wording below is written specifically for this revision website and follows the Clarity disclosure guidance you shared.

1. Site Disclosure

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how this revision site is used and to improve our lessons, question bank, document viewers and overall site experience. By using this site, you agree that we and Microsoft may collect and use behavioral data for analytics and improvement.

2. What We Collect

Through Microsoft Clarity, we may collect behavioral metrics such as page views, clicks, scrolls, navigation paths, device and browser information, session replay activity and heatmap data. This data helps us understand which content is useful, which pages confuse learners and where the site may need fixing.

3. Why We Use It

  • To improve lessons, examples and revision explanations.
  • To see which strands and pages are used most often.
  • To improve navigation, scrolling behavior and question-bank usability.
  • To identify technical issues, low-performing pages and possible abuse or security problems.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Microsoft Clarity may use first-party and third-party cookies and similar tracking technologies to measure site activity, understand popularity, support analytics and improve the performance and safety of the website.

5. Microsoft Disclosure

We partner with Microsoft Clarity to capture how visitors use and interact with this website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps and session replay. This information is used for site optimization, content improvement, analytics, fraud and security review and understanding what parts of the site are most useful.

For more information about how Microsoft collects and uses data, read the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

6. Sensitive Information

This site is designed for revision and study, not for collecting sensitive personal information. Do not submit passwords, payment details or other confidential personal information through this website.
