Strand 3 - Chapter 3

Security and Web Technologies

This chapter joins information security with safe web use. It covers the CIA triad, threats, protection methods, cyberbullying, safe searching, website evaluation, VLEs and blogs.

Start Chapter Question Bank

Main Ideas

  1. Confidentiality, integrity and availability
  2. Interruption, interception, modification and fabrication
  3. Cyberbullying, cyberstalking and digital footprint
  4. Search techniques and website evaluation
  5. Blogs and virtual learning environments

CIA Triad

  • Confidentiality: keep data private from unauthorised users.
  • Integrity: keep data correct and trustworthy.
  • Availability: make sure data and services are accessible when needed.

Major Threats and Protections

Threat Meaning Protection
Interruption Data or service becomes unavailable Backup and redundancy
Interception Wrong people see the data Encryption and authentication
Modification Data is changed wrongly Access control and integrity checks
Fabrication False data is created Authorisation and auditing

Online Safety Issues

  • Cyberbullying: repeated online harassment.
  • Cyberstalking: online tracking or harassment that causes fear.
  • Digital footprint: traces you leave online.
  • Digital shadow: data about you collected by others.
  • Hacking techniques: phishing, keylogging, eavesdropping and denial of service.

Legal Issues

  • Copyright protects original work.
  • Patent protects inventions.
  • Trademark protects brand names or symbols.
  • Piracy is the illegal copying or use of protected work.

Searching Well

  • Use correct spelling and exact terms.
  • Use AND to narrow results.
  • Use OR to widen results.
  • Use NOT to remove unwanted terms.
  • Compare results from more than one search engine.

Website Evaluation

  • Accuracy
  • Credibility
  • Content
  • Current
  • Functionality

Virtual Learning Environments

  • VLEs support learning outside the normal classroom.
  • They allow self-paced revision and access to online lessons.
  • Examples of open learning sites include Khan Academy and Coursera.

Blogs

  • A blog is a regularly updated website or web page.
  • Blogs can be used by schools, clubs or individuals.
  • Basic steps: choose a platform, plan content, create posts, publish and invite comments.
